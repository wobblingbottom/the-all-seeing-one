Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy covers the Crazyland Discord bot and its dashboard at bot.crazyland.art, operated by Crazyland ("we").

Data we process

  • Discord information: user and server IDs, usernames, avatars, roles, channels, and permissions needed to operate the bot and check dashboard access.
  • Activity and economy: XP, levels, activity timestamps, virtual balances, rewards, purchases, inventory, cooldowns, and transaction or audit records.
  • Moderation: user and moderator IDs, actions, reasons, timestamps, warning history, and message links supplied by moderators or recorded by honeypot actions.
  • Server settings: configured channels, roles, message templates, shop items, and feature rules.
  • Dashboard sessions: Discord sign-in information and an OAuth access token held in a signed session cookie to maintain your login and check server access. Sessions expire after seven days. We do not receive your Discord password.
  • Premium: Discord user and server IDs, PayPal subscription and plan IDs, subscription status, approval links, and access expiry. PayPal processes payment details; the application does not store card numbers.

The bot reads message content and attachment metadata to apply configured activity filters and moderation features. It does not store a general archive of message bodies or attachment files. Moderation reasons, settings, and logs can contain text supplied by users or server administrators.

Why we use data

We use this information to provide leveling, economy, rewards, moderation, dashboards, and subscriptions; verify permissions; prevent abuse; and investigate service errors. Where applicable, we rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing it, and legal obligations. Where consent is required, we will request it.

Who can see it

Leaderboards, rewards, and bot responses may display member information to others in the server. Authorized server administrators can access settings and moderation records. Configured Discord log channels receive relevant moderation and economy records, and affected users may receive DMs.

Discord processes bot interactions and sign-in, and PayPal processes subscriptions when used. Hosting providers process data needed to run the service. We may disclose information where required by law. We do not sell Discord user data or use message content to train AI models. See Discord's Privacy Policy and PayPal's Privacy Statement.

Storage and retention

Bot records are stored in the service's database. Most records have no automatic expiry; they remain until removed through available controls or a deletion request. Leaving a server, removing the bot, or clearing a warning does not automatically erase all historical records. We retain data only while needed for the service, security, dispute handling, or legal obligations, and delete it when no longer necessary. Discord messages and logs are separate copies controlled by the relevant server and Discord.

Your choices and rights

You can log out of the dashboard, revoke the application in Discord's Authorized Apps settings, and ask server administrators about server-specific settings and logs. Administrators can disable features or remove the bot.

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or object to processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, use the contact instructions below and include your Discord user ID and relevant server IDs. We may ask you to verify ownership. Revoking access or leaving a server does not itself submit a deletion request.

Children

The service is for people who meet Discord's minimum age and the minimum age required in their country. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. Contact us if you believe this has happened.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion, email crazyland.support@gmail.com. Include your Discord user ID and relevant server IDs. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or payment card details.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes. Review it when using new features.
